# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and equitable community for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, views, and experiences
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for our actions and correcting our mistakes
- Respecting the boundaries of others
- Prioritizing not only our own lives but also the well-being of others
- Accepting responsibility for our impact, whether positive or negative

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or secondhand attacks
- Harassment or intimidation, public or private
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit consent
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and to communicate directly to individuals when their behavior is deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Community leaders have the responsibility to communicate with individuals who they deem to be in violation of our Code of Conduct, to explain why their behavior is inappropriate, and to work toward a resolution that benefits the entire community.

## Enforcement Principles

Community leaders will follow these community impact guidelines when determining the severity of any inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful behavior:

- **Community Impact**: Actions that exclude, disrespect, or ignore others may be considered harmful to the community.
- **Corrective Impact**: Actions that correct the harmful behavior of others may be considered beneficial to the community.
- **Community Growth**: Actions that encourage community members to continue their participation may be considered beneficial to the community.
- **Community Safety**: Actions that protect the community from harm may be considered beneficial to the community.

Community leaders will apply these community impact principles flexibly when determining the severity of any inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful behavior.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or inappropriate behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct by emailing [conduct@example.com](mailto:conduct@example.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when individuals are officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community in public places include:

- Using an official community email address
- Posting via an official social media account
- Acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event
- Using an official official social media account
- Using an official email address
- Publishing in an official publication
